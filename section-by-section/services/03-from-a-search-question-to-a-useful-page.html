How we work / SEO visibility

From a search question to a useful page.

SEO visibility connects technical access, helpful content and relevant authority. Here is how we organize the work around your website.

  1. 01

    Find the visibility gaps

    Review your important pages, search intent and crawl or indexation issues. Agree on the priorities before delivery.

  2. 02

    Build useful content

    Map relevant topics to the right pages, then create or improve content that answers your audience’s questions.

  3. 03

    Connect content and authority

    Improve internal links and identify relevant publishing opportunities, with placements documented against the agreed scope.

  4. 04

    Review the search signals

    Compare Search Console clicks, impressions and page performance over time. Use the evidence to guide the next priorities.

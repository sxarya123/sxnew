How to take part

Start with context. Then find the right conversation.

  1. Tell people what your site covers. Niche, audience and the pages you are working on help others judge relevance.
  2. Compare ideas before links. A collaboration should make editorial sense for readers on both websites.
  3. Follow the channel rules. The subreddit requires website links and SEO service promotions to be introduced in Discord first.
